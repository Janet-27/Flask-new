The core idea

The logic looks at two moving averages of the stock price:

50-day moving average (50 DMA) – short-term trend

200-day moving average (200 DMA) – long-term trend

It also looks at the slope (direction) of the 50 DMA, i.e., whether it’s going up or down.

By comparing the position of 50 DMA vs 200 DMA and whether 50 DMA is rising or falling, your app assigns the stock to one of four stages:

The four stages
Stage	Condition	Intuition
Stage 1 – Accumulation	50 DMA < 200 DMA and slope of 50 DMA > 0	The stock is below its long-term trend but starting to rise. Smart investors may be quietly buying.
Stage 2 – Uptrend	50 DMA > 200 DMA and slope of 50 DMA > 0	The stock is above its long-term trend and rising – clear bullish trend.
Stage 3 – Distribution	50 DMA > 200 DMA and slope of 50 DMA < 0	The stock is still above its long-term trend but starts slowing down or falling – smart investors may be selling.
Stage 4 – Decline	50 DMA < 200 DMA and slope of 50 DMA < 0	The stock is below its long-term trend and falling – bearish trend.
In simple words

Below long-term trend, rising → Accumulation (buy quietly)

Above long-term trend, rising → Uptrend (bullish)

Above long-term trend, falling → Distribution (sell or take profits)

Below long-term trend, falling → Decline (bearish)

✅ Essentially, it’s a momentum + trend position strategy:

Where the stock is (above/below 200 DMA) → trend

Where it’s going (slope of 50 DMA) → momentum

Combine them → one of four stages.
